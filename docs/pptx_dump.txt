=== SLIDE 2 ===
THE PROBLEM
Every Minute Costs Lives in an Emergency
A delay of even 60–90 seconds can be fatal in cardiac-arrest cases — yet emergency vehicles in Indian and global cities still fight the same avoidable obstacles.
1
Heavy Urban Congestion
Dense peak-hour traffic physically blocks emergency paths, even when drivers intend to give way.
2
Driver Unawareness
Sirens and beacons are often unheard inside sound-insulated cars or masked by loud music.
3
Static Traffic Signals
Fixed-cycle signals cannot react to an approaching emergency vehicle, causing needless stops.
4
No Coordinated Routing
Drivers rely on manual judgment instead of real-time, AI-optimised route selection.
5
No Predictive Capability
Existing GPS tools show current jams but cannot anticipate the ones forming minutes later.
6
No Multi-Vehicle Priority
When two emergency vehicles converge on one intersection, there is no protocol to resolve it.

=== SLIDE 4 ===
RESEARCH FOUNDATION
Three Published Studies Shape the AI Design
Each subsystem of LifeLane AI builds on a peer-reviewed contribution rather than a from-scratch method.
TRAFFIC FORECASTING
Graph-and-Attention Network for Traffic Flow Prediction
Combines seasonal preprocessing, spatiotemporal attention, and an Adaptive Graph Convolutional Network with LSTM to forecast congestion using road-network topology, not just history.
Lowest MAE / RMSE of 7 compared methods, with fewer parameters than larger rivals.
Source: Xiao, Zou & Xiao (2025), Scientific Reports
VEHICLE DETECTION
Cloud-Based Ambulance Detection for Signal Priority
A single-stage YOLOv8 detector, trained on a self-built 3,000-image, 10-country dataset, runs on a 4-layer cloud pipeline to recognise ambulances and trigger a signal's green phase.
98.2% accuracy, 95.8% recall on the pre-trained universal model.
Source: Noor et al. (2024), Applied Sciences (MDPI)
ROUTE & EVACUATION
Salp-Swarm Path Planning with Dynamic Evacuation
A two-stage optimiser: a Hybrid Salp Swarm Algorithm with Dijkstra proposes alternative shortest paths, then an Improved SSA reroutes background traffic under a congestion-index constraint.
24.22% average travel-time cut, 30–59% faster convergence than rival metaheuristics.
Source: Duan, Wu & Xiong (2022), Journal of Advanced Transportation

=== SLIDE 7 ===
METHODOLOGY · PART 1 OF 2
From Registration to Green Corridor
1
Emergency Registration
Driver presses START EMERGENCY; GPS, destination, speed and heading stream to the cloud every 500 ms under a new emergency session ID.
2
Live Traffic Data Collection
The AI engine fuses five live sources: maps APIs, CCTV vehicle counts, IoT road sensors, weather alerts, and historical traffic patterns.
3
AI Traffic Prediction
An LSTM + Graph Neural Network forecasts congestion 5–10 minutes ahead per road segment, re-scored every 30 seconds.
4
Smart Route Optimisation
A modified Dijkstra / A* search weighs travel time, predicted congestion, signal wait, road quality and intersection count to pick the fastest route in under 200 ms.
5
Green Corridor Creation
The Signal Control Module times each intersection so its light turns green 5–8 seconds before the vehicle arrives, then reverts within 10 seconds after passing.

=== SLIDE 8 ===
METHODOLOGY · PART 2 OF 2
From Driver Alerts to Live Command
6
Intelligent Vehicle Notification
Drivers within 500 m are alerted 30–45 seconds ahead via navigation push, roadside LED boards, V2X broadcasts, and FM radio interrupt.
7
Traffic Diversion
The AI Diversion Engine reroutes normal traffic to parallel roads and lengthens their green phase, capping added delay near 90 seconds per vehicle.
8
Dynamic Signal Timing
Green-start time is computed from live distance and speed, advancing or extending as the vehicle speeds up or slows — capped at 45 seconds.
9
Real-Time Route Recalculation
A background service re-checks for accidents, closures or faster routes every 10 seconds and issues a rerouted corridor within 3 seconds.
10
Emergency Control Dashboard
Traffic police view a live map, ETA, signal status, CCTV feeds and a real-time 'Time Saved' counter versus standard navigation.
