## Security, Anti-Spoofing & Operational Gap Resolutions (Verified)

1. **G5 (Auth & Privacy):**
   - JWT authentication enforced across REST endpoints and WebSockets (`/ws/telemetry/{session_id}?token=...` and `/ws/police?token=...`).
   - Automated 30-day telemetry retention purge implemented (`backend/app/services/telemetry_manager.py:purge_stale_telemetry` and `DELETE /api/v1/telemetry/purge`).
   - Privacy and data retention policy clearly disclosed in site footer and cookie consent banner.

2. **G6 (Anti-Spoofing & Misuse Prevention):**
   - Driver verification gate: emergency sessions require approved operator status (`PATCH /api/v1/auth/drivers/{driver_id}/approve` and database check on `POST /emergency/sessions`). Unapproved drivers cannot initiate green corridors.
   - GPS telemetry rate-limiting: incoming frames throttled to $\le 10\text{ frames/sec}$ (HTTP 429 upon violation).
   - Physics-based anomaly filter: speed jumps $> 120\text{ km/h}$ or acceleration $> 8.0\text{ m/s}^2$ flagged as `is_anomaly=True` with `SPOOF_ANOMALY_WARNING` broadcast to police console.

3. **G7 (Fail-Safe Reversion Protocols):**
   - **Post-Passage Reversion:** Within 10 seconds after emergency vehicle crosses an intersection ($< 15\text{ m}$ or passed along route), the signal transitions to `POST_PASSAGE_REVERT` and reverts to standard cyclic timing.
   - **Stale Telemetry Timeout:** If active vehicle telemetry stops for $> 3.0\text{ s}$, all active corridor signals immediately fail-safe to cyclic RED (`STALE_FAILSAFE_REVERT`), preventing city-wide gridlock.
   - **Driver Google Maps Exit Warning:** Mandatory 3-second reversion warning prompted if the driver switches to external Google Maps navigation.

4. **G17 (Driver Registration & Review Workflow):**
   - Expiring, limited-use QR registration tokens generated by police/admin.
   - Pillow EXIF-stripping, magic bytes validation, and non-blocking background OCR extraction.
   - 8 automated consistency checks (number format, state code, number mismatch, name fuzzy match, expiry, issue plausibility, image quality, duplicates) for human review.

5. **G18 (Live Navigation & Along-Route Corridor):**
   - Async OSRM client with 3.0s timeout and straight-line fallback.
   - Along-route corridor preemption: signals ordered by sequence of encounter along route polyline rather than straight-line radius.
   - Real GPS toggle (`navigator.geolocation.watchPosition`) and polyline dev simulator in driver HUD.
